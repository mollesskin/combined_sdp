# DoS study: brief summary

All four original files were reviewed and executed: two full V0–V3 comparisons (`dos_address`, `dos_number`), one single-fix comparison (`dos_simple`), and one comparison of alternative workflows (`auction`). There are no unresolved cases within the declared scope. The full engineering account and limitations are in the original `README.md`; code, protocol, raw outputs, and CSV files remain in the experiment directory.

At a load of 5,950 records, A accepts and clears all 5,950 in 17 cleanup steps. B and A+B accept 1,750, reject 4,200, and clear the accepted records in one and five steps respectively. The report retains the requested-work completion rate of 1,750/5,950: the cap is a capacity trade-off, not completion of the full request. The baseline atomic cleanup reverts at the 12-million-gas limit, makes no progress, and preserves the data. At 1,750 records, every condition completed the work and could start a new cycle. V3 did not improve on A or B: compared with A it reduced available capacity at high load, and compared with B it added steps and cost without improving completion.

`dos_simple` already has a maximum fill cycle of 1,750, so no new B mechanism was added. V0 and F1 completed the cycle within the fixed budget; F1 needed five cleanup calls instead of one and consumed 133,804 more gas. In the mining check, one transaction remained pending after the first block, but all four were included by the second block. This is neither permanent DoS nor an execution revert. Probes exceeding the block gas limit were separately rejected before inclusion.

For `auction`, PULL and FALLBACK are alternatives sharing deferred credit, with no artificial A+B condition. Both accept a 20-wei bid after a refusing bidder's 10-wei bid, preserve the 10-wei obligation when a claim fails, and then pay 10 wei to a willing recipient chosen by the claim owner. A repeated claim pays zero. V0 does not accept the new bid; the previous live 10-wei bid remains backed and is not described as erased refund debt.

The two primary clean runs matched in observations, outcomes, and status histories: 338 measured transactions and 34 variant-by-scenario records per run, plus eight mining diagnostic transactions and two probes. All 638 evidence checks passed. The preliminary v1 run remains separate: the correction retrieved a nested `transactionHash` for four out-of-gas receipts; it did not change the conditions, workloads, or contracts. Versions and exact commands are saved.

The 1,760 arithmetic files and 1,626 access-control files were checked read-only by hash. They were unchanged and were not rerun. PES was neither modified nor calculated. The implementation was AI-assisted; this is not assignment submission text. No new categories were started after DoS.
